What is ScamSense?

ScamSense is an educational browser-based tool created to help everyday internet users cultivate healthy skepticism and recognize common warning signs in suspicious messages, emails, and links.

Rather than relying on vague "AI black-box detection scores," ScamSense explains the concrete psychology behind deceptive communications: artificial urgency, fear tactics, unauthorized fee demands, and credential traps.

🔒

100% Client-Side

Everything runs directly in your browser. Your messages and notes are never sent to external servers or stored in any remote database.

💡

Actionable Guidance

Instead of panic, ScamSense provides simple verification steps so you always know what to check independently before taking action.

🎓

Educational Focus

Built as a learning companion that trains your intuition over time, turning you and your family into harder targets for scammers.

Important Safety Notice

ScamSense does not guarantee that a message is safe or fraudulent. It provides educational indicators that can help you decide what to verify. Always independently contact organizations through official bookmarks, app stores, or phone numbers printed on your billing statements.
